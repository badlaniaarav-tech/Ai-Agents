## Rules

1. **Cut straight to the swing, no long address.** Open mid-action; the guides say the hook must land in 1 to 2 seconds and warn against logos, slow zooms and silent intros. Metricool says the first three seconds get most of the attention. For a swing: start at or just before the takeaway, not on the golfer standing still. (A + C)
   Sources: Metricool Instagram Reels Guide, 21 Sep 2026; ReelKraft "How to Edit Instagram Reels That Go Viral", 6 Jun 2026 (older); a hook-advice result in the same search that said "avoid logos, slow zooms, or silent intros" (undated).

2. **Keep it short: 6 to 10 seconds, with a clean loop.** Sources conflict on length. Socialinsider (9.77M Reels, Jan to Jun 2026, brands 1K to 1M followers) shows the best median views at 45 to 60 s (10,374) and the lowest at 0 to 30 s (4,700), but that is brand content, mostly talking or story, not single swings. Metricool (21 Sep 2026) says average watch time is 8.5 s per Reel and that Reels watched to the end or replayed get pushed further. Several vendor blogs put best reach at 7 to 15 s (undated, unverified). A swing has nothing to say after 10 s, so completion and replays are the lever: short plus a loop. Avoid a hard stop: end on a frame that matches the first frame. (A + C)
   Sources: Socialinsider, published 14 Jul 2026, updated 20 Oct 2026; Metricool, 21 Sep 2026; ReelKraft loop technique, 6 Jun 2026 (older); SocialPilot, Sep 2026: core ranking order is watch time and completion, then sends per reach, then likes per reach.

3. **Make it sendable.** Sends per reach is the signal tied to reaching non-followers (Mosseri, Jan 2025, as repeated by SocialPilot Sep 2026; the 3x to 5x weight figure is not verified). One plain text prompt that invites a send or a rating works. Golf hook tool page suggests lines like "Rate this swing 1-10". Keep it plain, no hype. (A + B)
   Sources: SocialPilot "Instagram Reels Algorithm", Sep 2026; videohooks.app golf spin page (undated).

4. **Slow motion on the strike only, not the whole clip.** Sources say pick the most impactful moment and do not stretch footage, or the video drags. Instagram's built-in slow options are 0.3x and 0.5x; CapCut uses the speed slider plus Curves. Golf hook tool page recommends slow motion to emphasize the moment. Suggested starting point (Inference, test it): normal speed to the top of the backswing, ramp to 0.3x to 0.5x through impact, ramp back to normal on the follow-through. Film at 60 or 120 fps so the slow part is smooth. (A + B + C)
   Sources: CapCut slow-motion guide (undated); Instagram slow-mo guides via CapCut resource page (undated); videohooks.app (undated).

5. **Keep the real crack of the strike, loud, on the contact frame.** The sound-effect source says land the strike sound on the exact frame the club meets the ball, and use a whoosh before it to build anticipation. CapCut warns that slowing a clip distorts its audio, so detach the audio in the slowed part and rebuild it. Plan: keep the original strike sound at full level, sync it to contact, add a short whoosh in the slow part only if the original sounds thin. Do not run music loud through the strike. (B + C)
   Sources: Morphic golf swing sound page (undated vendor page); CapCut slow-motion guide (undated).

6. **Frame vertical 9:16, golfer large, text out of the screen edges.** Export 1080x1920. Keep key content and text out of the top 250 px and bottom 250 px where the username and caption sit (Metricool, 21 Sep 2026). Another guide says keep text above the bottom 15%. No source gave a best camera angle (down-the-line vs face-on) or how tight to crop; I could not find one. Inference, test it: frame the golfer and club head with room above the swing arc so the crop does not clip the finish; shoot down-the-line and face-on and compare view counts. (A + C)
   Sources: Metricool, 21 Sep 2026; Instagram-reel best-practice blog result (undated).

7. **Little or no text: one short line, big, high contrast, in the first second.** Many viewers watch without sound; self-test: watch the first 3 seconds muted and ask if you would keep watching. Burned-in caption lines of 3 to 5 words (ReelKraft, 6 Jun 2026, older), which matches our brand rule of 3 words per line. Golf edit accounts on TikTok use a plain Arial-heavy text style (SI, 23 Aug 2026), so a clean sans-serif is current, not fancy fonts. For a no-talking swing there is no caption to burn in, so text is a hook or a label (club, yardage, fault), not subtitles. Avoid: paragraphs, text over the swing path, text in the bottom band, stacked stickers. (A + B)
   Sources: Metricool, 21 Sep 2026; SI "Next phase golf online takeover moment on TikTok fan edits", 23 Aug 2026; ReelKraft, 6 Jun 2026 (older).

8. **Music: trending sound under the clip is a reach tool; original audio is the safe default for a swing.** Golf fan-edit accounts (highlight edits of pros, a different format) use high-energy trending tracks, often pop (SI, 23 Aug 2026). Metricool (21 Sep 2026) says Reels using a sound can surface in that sound's discovery flow. Creator blogs say trending audio gives a short boost (about 48 hours) while original audio holds better for saves and shares, and that completion and reshares matter more than audio type (undated, unverified). No source gave a volume number for a clip with no voice. Plan: post with the real strike sound; add a trending sound inside Instagram at low volume if it does not cover the crack. (A + B)
   Sources: SI, 23 Aug 2026; Metricool, 21 Sep 2026; Instagram best-practice blogs (undated).

9. **After the strike: hold the finish, then loop back.** Hold the finish pose about 1 second, then cut or loop to the start. No source measured finish holds. Ball-flight tracer: tracer apps exist (Golf Ball Tracer, Golf Shot Tracer Auto Track, Shot Tracer Map, App Store listings, undated) and export 9:16 for Reels, but I found no data showing tracers raise views. Use a tracer only if the ball is visible and the line is clean; skip it otherwise. Reaction and cut-to-result: not found as a measured pattern; our clips have no talking so skip. (B + C)
   Sources: App Store listings for the tracer apps (undated, developer marketing); ReelKraft loop technique, 6 Jun 2026 (older).

10. **One continuous shot is on trend, so the fixed camera is an asset.** Later's trend list (updated 16 Oct 2026) includes single-shot formats (for example "Getting robbed", 2 Oct 2026) and loop formats (for example "The summer schedule", 26 Jun 2026, hook repeated four times). They are not golf, but they show unedited single shots and visual loops are current. Do not add cuts just to look edited; one speed ramp and one text line is enough. (A)
    Source: Later "Top Instagram Reels Trends to Try in 2026", last updated 16 Oct 2026.

11. **Cover and first frame must read on mute.** Metricool (21 Sep 2026): custom covers should use high contrast and short text. Our brand rule already fits: 3 words max, all caps, last word yellow. Pick the cover frame at the top of the swing or the finish, with the golfer large. (A)
    Source: Metricool, 21 Sep 2026.

12. **Mistakes that kill views (and legal ones).** Sourced: long intros or silent starts, slow zooms, too much slow motion, stretched footage, text hidden under the interface, and watermarked or non-original content (Hootsuite advice attributed to Mosseri, via search summary, undated). Music risk: a golf edit account lost a video with over 1M views to a copyright strike (SI, 23 Aug 2026), and Metricool (21 Sep 2026) warns music fine for organic Reels may not be cleared for ads. (A + B + C)

## Recipe: 6 to 10 second single-swing reel

All of this follows rules 1 to 12; the speed and angle numbers are starting points to test, not proven values.

1. 0.0 to 0.3 s: start at address or takeaway, no black or title frame. One short text line (about 3 words, plain bold sans-serif, white with dark outline or shadow, upper third, clear of the top 250 px) such as the club or the swing thought.
2. 0.3 to 3 s: normal speed through backswing. Original room audio; no music over the strike.
3. Top of backswing to just after impact (about 1 s of real time): ramp to 0.3x to 0.5x. Strike crack at full level on the contact frame. Optional short whoosh before it. No flash, no zoom punch (Inference, test it).
4. After impact: ramp back to normal speed, hold the finish for about 1 s. Show a tracer only if the ball line is clean.
5. Last frame: match the first frame so the reel loops. Total 6 to 10 s.
6. Export 1080x1920, 9:16. Cover: 3 words max, all caps, last word in #FFD400, chosen frame with the golfer large.
7. Keep edit.mp4 without a trending sound so Aarav can add one inside Instagram, per the brand music rule.
8. Test: post two angles (down-the-line vs face-on) and two lengths and compare watch time, replays and sends in Metricool.

## Five rules to add to the brand as "Reel style"

1. A swing reel is 6 to 10 seconds, starts at the takeaway with no standing around, and ends on a frame that loops back to the start.
2. Slow motion only through the strike (about 0.3x to 0.5x), back to normal speed after impact; no flash, no shaky zoom.
3. Keep the real strike sound loud and lined up with the contact frame; music never covers it.
4. Text is one short line, big, plain bold sans-serif, in the first second, and stays out of the top and bottom 250 pixels.
5. Frame vertical 9:16 with the golfer large; test down-the-line against face-on and keep the angle that gets more watch time and sends.
