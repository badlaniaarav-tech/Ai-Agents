## 1. Moods

### hype
- Tempo: 130 to 150 BPM (phonk and trap), or 85 to 100 BPM half-time (rap).
- Feel: hard bass, cowbell or 808, a drop you can cut on.
- Seen on: golf fan edits use rap or house with flash transitions (SI). On the charts now: "DIP ON EM" by SouthMemphisHypeman (TikTok, rising, flagged hype), "Jukiar" by Clarent and Quevedo (Instagram, flagged sports and hype).
- Use for: big drive, long-drive, trick shot, hole-out, any clip with a clear impact moment. Put the drop on the ball strike.

### chill
- Tempo: 70 to 95 BPM.
- Feel: soft lo-fi beat, warm keys or acoustic, low volume.
- Seen on: "October" by Adrian Berenguer (Instagram, 811K posts, peak), "Autumn Journey" by Andrea Vanzo, "My October" by The Folks, "Where To Find Me" by Cedar Creek Kitchen (TikTok, flagged for technique close-ups).
- Use for: range sessions, lessons, tips with talking, practice routines, course walks. Keep it quiet so the club sound and your voice stay clear.

### cinematic
- Tempo: 60 to 100 BPM, slow build, or 100 to 130 BPM for intense trailer style.
- Feel: low pads, big hit, slow rise. Good for slow motion.
- Seen on: "STORM II" by Gener8ion and Yung Lean (Instagram, 90K posts, rising, flagged cinematic and hype), "Patient Zero" by Taylor Swift (dramatic milestone moments), "Kiss of Life" by Sade (cinematic everyday edits, Sept).
- Use for: slow-motion swing, ball flight, sunrise or golden-hour rounds, "swing change" reveals.

### funny
- Tempo: 100 to 130 BPM, bouncy, or no music with a sound effect.
- Feel: quirky pizzicato, chaotic, short punch.
- Seen on: "Whack" shank sound (golfers, fails, since 2021), "Bounce it" by Biggs money (TikTok, 260.5K videos, flagged hype and funny), "Grr Mondays" from Ted (deadpan, Oct 7 and 14), "Windows in Brazil (Sped Up)" (playful).
- Use for: fails, shanks, bad lies, club throws, friends-lose-a-bet clips.

### pop (extra mood, data says so)
- Tempo: 100 to 125 BPM (disco, pop, funk).
- Feel: bright, retro groove, sing-along.
- Seen on: golf editor @golf_brat uses Billboard pop (SI, Aug 23). Charts: "Upside Down" by Diana Ross (Instagram, 175K reels, transition reveals), "Funkytown" by Lipps Inc. (rapid-cut montages), "Ring My Bell" by Anita Ward (Sept).
- Use for: before-and-after swing fixes, outfit and course montages, "day in my golf life", beat-synced transitions.
