Find out what music the viral videos in our niche are using right now, so our edits sound like what is working.

1. Read the brain (`mcp__memory__open_nodes` ["Company", "Brand rules"]) for the niche and the music rule. Topic for this run: $ARGUMENTS (default: the company's niche; for Aarav that is golf swing reels and shorts on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube).
2. Start a **trend-scout** sub-agent with this brief:
   - Search the last 60 days for the viral short videos in the niche (reels, TikToks, shorts with high views) and what audio they use: trending sounds, genres, tempo (BPM), mood, whether the music sits under a voice or carries the clip alone. Use creator roundups, "trending audio this week" posts, TikTok Creative Center and Instagram reels trend pages where reachable.
   - Group what you find into 3 to 5 moods (for example `hype`, `chill`, `cinematic`, `funny`) with tempo, feel and example sounds for each, and when each mood fits (big drive, range session, slow-motion swing, fail).
   - For every mood, find 2 or 3 royalty-free tracks that sound similar, with the download link, from libraries we may use: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, Uppbeat, Free Music Archive, Epidemic Sound or Artlist if the owner has a subscription. The owner downloads them into `music/` as `<mood>-<name>.mp3`.
   - List the 5 trending sounds themselves (name, artist, where it is trending) for the owner to add inside the Instagram or TikTok app over `edit.mp4`; we never download or copy those.
   - Write it to `music/brief.md` with the date at the top, these sections: Moods, Tracks to download (file name to use, link), Trending sounds to add in-app, Sources. Short lines, plain words.
3. Check `ls music/` and say which moods already have a track and which still need one.
4. Report to the owner: the moods, the tracks to download (with links), and the trending sounds for in-app use. Nothing is downloaded or posted by the office.
